Taking a Pom to a dog park

  • Fenced parks are the safer pick for a dog that fits through a gap. Only parks tagged as fenced in OpenStreetMap carry the Fenced label, so check the fence line yourself on the first visit.
  • Go at quiet times first and watch how the bigger dogs play before you let your Pom in.
  • Bring the harness, not just a collar. The harness training guide helps if yours hates putting it on.
  • Count the park time as part of the day. The daily walk guide shows what owners report.
