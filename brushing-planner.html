Build a brushing schedule

Pick a coat stage, the season and the time you have. Start with a week you can keep, then adapt it to your dog.

Nothing is stored or sent. Results update as you change a setting. This is an adjustable planning aid, not a measured breed-wide schedule.

Your plan

Adult coat · Normal season

Four sessions a week, up to 10 minutes each.

  • MonLine brush body; comb ruff10 min
  • WedRear and trousers; check feet10 min
  • FriBrush through the coat; finish with a comb10 min
  • SunGentle check behind ears and harness areas5 min
Do not skip week 7

Use week seven of your own plan as a reminder to reassess the routine. Coat changes vary; this is not a claim that every puppy changes coat at seven weeks or seven months.

Read the routine behind the planner

The day allocations are an editable starting point. Session length is a time budget: stop sooner if your dog has had enough. Puppy practice and coat checks may need shorter sessions between these four days.
