05 / How we work

From owner posts to your answer

  1. Collect

    We read public owner threads and keep only the posts that describe a real dog.

  2. Count

    Each report is coded the same way, so you see a median and a range, not one loud anecdote.

  3. Compare

    Owner numbers sit next to the breed standard or a published chart, with links to both.

  4. Check

    Every guide ends in a small tool, so you can place your own Pom in about two minutes.

Pomeranian Guide is written with AI assistance and checked by a person. We are not a vet clinic: guides describe what owners report and what published sources say, and send you to a vet whenever home care is not enough. Read the editorial policy.
